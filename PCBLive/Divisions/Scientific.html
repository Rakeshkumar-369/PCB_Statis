Scientific Division


Vision of the Scientific Division

“To be a Referal Laboratory where reference samples any where from India can be analysed”

Board Laboratories

The Board has a Central Laboratory, 4 Zonal Laboratories viz., Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Kurnool and Tirupati headed by Senior Environmental Scientist (SES). The Board also has one Regional Laboratory at Kakinada. The Laboratories undertake activities of monitoring and analysis of air, water, wastewater, soil and hazardous waste samples.

The Central Laboratory coordinates the activities of Zonal & Regional Laboratories and plays a key role in dissemination of data acquired from monitoring activities under various CPCB projects viz., NAMP & NWMP. Several other projects are being carried out at Zonal & Regional Laboratories viz., COMAPS, Kolleru lake monitoring, etc. the data of which is useful in understanding the well-being of water bodies and fresh water ecosystems.

The Zonal Laboratories are equipped with state-of-the-art technology to carry out monitoring and analysis of environmental samples under Water, Air and EP Acts. The monitoring & analysis activities are dealt by well trained, experienced and competent Environmental Scientists (Board Analysts) of the Board.
